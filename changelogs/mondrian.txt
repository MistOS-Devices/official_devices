- Added recoverable resolution handling with density preservation, preview, rollback and reset support

- Fixed BootAnimation positioning and scaling after logical display resolution changes

- Implemented Haptic Engine support with additional vibration effects and improved feedback closer to HyperOS
- Added full LHDC support
- Updated and improved Dolby integration

- Added additional audio stack improvements for proper Dolby and LHDC functionality

- Improved screen recording stability and fixed issues related to saving recordings
- Reworked auto-brightness configuration for smoother transitions and fixed brightness slider freezing
- Completely reworked powerhint configuration for a better balance between performance and battery life
- Enabled Sustained Performance Mode for more stable frame rates and thermal behavior under prolonged heavy workloads
- Fixed Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax) connection drops and improved WPA3 transitional mode compatibility
- Retained all previous Android 16 device-tree features and optimizations in the Android 17 tree
